**HiveMind 0.1.0.5** — long-range reserve loadouts, safe specialized shulker restocking and bot bases for **Minecraft 26.3 / Fabric**.

- **Rockets:** keep roughly 128–256, refilling below 128. **Food:** keep roughly 64–128 allowed food items, refilling below 64. **Elytras:** keep two usable wings total, including an equipped elytra. **Totems:** keep three to four total, including the offhand; refill below three.
- Carried shulkers can be specialized by contents. The bot inspects client-known contents, places a useful box, withdraws the needed category, breaks and confirms pickup of its own box, then uses another box as needed. Rocket variants and mixed allowed foods count together. Ordinary deposits retain portable boxes, rockets and totems along with equipment and food.
- Portable restocking requires a continuous five-second quiet period on safe ground, with no nearby untrusted players/hostile mobs or active conflict. Threats pause interactions. The recovery slot stays reserved. Flight can request a purposeful landing for available carried reserves away from nearby untrusted players; emergency escapes are not interrupted for restocking.
- **#botbase set** saves the current location and registers nearby loaded normal, trapped, barrel and copper/waxed/oxidized copper storage within 16 blocks. Existing storage rules are preserved; newly discovered containers receive a supplies label and priority. Radius is configurable from 4 to 32 blocks.
- **#botbase resupply** refills the loadout while preserving the current task. A configured but undiscovered base is visited to scan its stock. Native Overworld/Nether portal routes are used when known, and base trips return to the interrupted location. Missing portals, blocked storage, missing stock and inventory limits remain visible in status.
- **#botbase status**, **scan**, and **clear** manage the anchor. **#bot base** is an alias. Clearing the anchor retains registered storage; remove unwanted containers through existing deposit commands.
- Controller cards show current reserve totals. Loadout targets are configurable under **Loadout**: botExpeditionRockets, botExpeditionFood, botExpeditionElytras, botExpeditionTotems. Old smaller rocket/totem policies are migrated so they do not override the requested reserve bands.

To assign a shared base through the controller, select all bots and send **#botbase set minecraft:overworld 100 64 -200 16** using your coordinates. Discovery happens when each bot has the relevant chunks loaded. The base does not create supplies; stock the chests or carry filled shulkers.

Update clients with **#update install** and relaunch Minecraft. Replace the standalone controller application with this release while retaining its data directory, then refresh the webpage for Loadout settings and totals. The live network protocol remains 7.
